Trading is one of the fastest ways to build up a strong Blox Fruits inventory, but it's also where most new players get taken advantage of. Someone offers a fruit you've never heard of for three of yours, throws in a rushed "trust me, it's worth way more," and suddenly you're not sure if you just made a great deal or gave away half your account for nothing.

This Blox Fruits trade calculator exists to take the guesswork out of that moment. Instead of scrolling through a Discord server hoping someone answers your "is this a good trade" question in time, you add both sides of the deal here and get an immediate breakdown: who's ahead, by how much, and whether the gap is big enough to actually matter. It works the same way experienced traders think about a deal — by adding up value on both sides and checking the size of the gap — just faster, and without anyone's opinion getting in the way.

Below the calculator you'll find a full value list covering the fruits, swords, guns, and accessories traders ask about most, each tagged with a rarity and a demand rating. Demand matters just as much as raw value: a mid-tier fruit that everyone wants can be easier to re-trade than a rare one nobody's asking for. Use both numbers together, not just one, when you're deciding whether to accept.

      Frequently asked questions

      How does the calculator decide win or lose?+
      It adds up the value of every item on your side and every item on the other side, then compares the totals. A side worth noticeably more comes out ahead; close totals are marked fair, using a small tolerance band around equal value.
      Are these official Blox Fruits prices?+
      No — Blox Fruits doesn't publish official trade prices. These numbers are community-style estimates meant as a sensible starting point. Real trading value shifts with updates, events, and community sentiment, so treat this as a reference, not a guarantee.
      What does "demand" mean here?+
      Demand is how many traders currently want an item — Low, Medium, High, or Very High. A high-value item with low demand can sit unsold longer than a cheaper item everyone wants, so it's worth weighing alongside raw value.
      Why isn't my item on the list?+
      The list covers the items traders ask about most. It's maintained by hand rather than pulled from a live feed, so brand-new releases may take a little while to be added.
      Is this site affiliated with Blox Fruits or Roblox?+
      No. This is an independent fan-made tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Blox Fruits developers or Roblox Corporation.
      What's the difference between an item's price and its trade value?+
      Price is what an item costs to buy directly in-game with Robux or Beli, if it's even purchasable that way. Value is what the trading community treats it as being worth in a swap — and the two numbers often don't match at all. A gamepass fruit might have a fixed purchase price but trade well above or below that depending on how much demand it currently has.
      Does a permanent fruit trade differently than a normal one?+
      Yes. A permanent (gamepass-purchased) fruit generally trades higher than the same fruit obtained as a normal in-game drop, since it can't be lost on death and carries more long-term value. When you're comparing offers, check whether either side's fruit is permanent before assuming the values are equal.
      How do I avoid getting scammed while trading?+
      Run the trade through this calculator first so you're not relying on someone else's word. Beyond that: never hand over an item before the in-game trade window confirms both sides, be suspicious of anyone rushing you or claiming a "glitch" means they need your item first, and don't log into third-party sites that promise free items — they're a common way accounts get stolen.
      How often are the values updated?+
      The list is maintained by hand rather than pulled from a live market feed, so it reflects a snapshot in time rather than minute-by-minute shifts. Big game updates, new fruit releases, and shifts in community sentiment are the usual triggers for a value to move — cross-check with an active trading community if a trade is high-stakes.
